# O7.7: bounds-check elimination in loops

- lane: aye
- goal: leave out the bounds, slice and induction-variable overflow checks that the code before them proves (`for i in 0..s.len { s[i] }`, `while i < s.len { s[i]; i += 1 }`, `for i, x in xs { xs[i] }`, a second `s[i]`, `s[i..]` after `i < s.len`), with no change to what panics.
- serves: O11–O13 (numbers), from aye-lang/aye#25 (Dweller Q0.2/Q1.1: `--mode safe` costs +17.6% on jsbench, navier-stokes 0.66 of fast mode)
- cause: every `x[i]` and `x[lo..hi]` was lowered with its check (`Proj::Index`, `Rvalue::Slice` in `src/oir.rs`), and `i += 1` with `__builtin_add_overflow`, even right after `i < s.len`.
- choice:
  - `src/bce.rs`, run at the end of `oir::lower` in every mode: `Proj::Index` and `Rvalue::Slice` gain `checked`, which `src/cgen.rs` reads; a proved `i + 1` / `i - 1` becomes `+%` / `-%`.
  - Facts (`i < b`, `x == term`, `c ⇒ i < b`) name only locals that nothing but an assignment changes: no globals, no local whose storage has its address taken (`&x`, `&x.f`, a method's `&mut x`, a slice of an array in `x`). They come from branch conditions (`<`, `<=`, `>`, `>=`, through `&&`, `||`, `!`), passed checks and copies (`let n = s.len`, the copy `for x in xs` makes). An assignment ends the facts naming its local; a loop starts with the entry facts naming no local it assigns (no fixpoint); a branch joins both ends. Checks in `@setSafety(false)` give no facts. At most 128 facts at a point.
  - No facts through pointers (`self.items[i]` keeps its check): a store or a call may change memory, and Aye has no type-based aliasing rule. Recorded in `docs/decisions.md` ("Proved checks are left out"); `docs/spec.md` section 16 says that a check that cannot fail is left out; practices entry "Index a local slice in a loop" in `docs/practices/aye.md`.

  At `-O2` clang already proves what the pass proves (the locals are SSA values to it), so safe builds barely change; debug builds and the C do.
- why jsbench does not move (callgrind, navier-stokes `SMOKE=10`): 77% of the instructions are in Dweller's `vmLoop`, whose indexes come from bytecode (`regs[(base + vmImm(bytes, pc, w)) as usize]`), not loops. Leaving out one kind of check at a time (experiment compilers, not committed): no bounds checks 2.026 G, no cast checks 2.250 G, no unwrap / `unreachable` checks 2.309 G, no overflow checks 2.363 G, against safe 2.312 G and fast 1.609 G. With no bounds check at all, safe still runs 1.26 times fast's instructions, so the issue's "navier-stokes safe/fast at or above 0.95" cannot come from bounds-check elimination.
