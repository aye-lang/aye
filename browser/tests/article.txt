Notes on Ore

By someone, October 6

Ore is a systems language without garbage collection. Memory comes from explicit Allocator values.

Why another language?

- Errors are values.
- Enums are tagged unions.
- No hidden allocation.

Simple things should be simple.

fn main() {
    @println("hi")
}
