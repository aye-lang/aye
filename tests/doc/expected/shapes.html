perimeter

pub fn perimeter(w: i64, h: i64) i64

The sum of the sides of a "box" with width `w` and height `h`.

Example:

perimeter(2, 3) == 10

Or fenced:

let p = perimeter(1, 1)

@expect(p == 4)
